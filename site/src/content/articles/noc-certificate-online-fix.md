## Quick Answers

| Problem | Common cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Citizenship verification fails | NID/old citizenship mismatch | Re-enter details exactly as on the document |
| Documents rejected | Unclear scans | Use clean scans, correct file types and sizes |
| Payment not going through | Gateway timeout | Retry after 10 minutes, try another payment method |


## Step 1: Know your purpose

1. NOC for study or employment has different forms — choose the right one.
2. Check the receiving institution's requirement (many universities need the NOC stamp).
3. Keep your admission letter or job offer ready.

## Step 2: Verify your identity details

1. Enter your name, DOB, and citizenship/NID number exactly as printed.
2. A single mismatch fails verification — do not use short forms.
3. If you hold old-style citizenship, use the number format shown on the portal.

## Step 3: Upload clean documents

1. Scan documents (front and back) in good light — no shadows.
2. Use the file format and size the portal asks for (usually JPEG/PDF).
3. Name files clearly (e.g., citizenship_front.jpg) to avoid mix-ups.

## Step 4: Pay the processing fee

1. Pay via the portal using eSewa, Khalti, or bank options.
2. Complete within the session time to avoid token expiry.
3. Save the receipt — you need it to collect the certificate.

## Step 5: Track and collect

1. Status updates appear on the portal (processing, approved, ready).
2. Once approved, collect in person with ID and the receipt, or follow the dispatch option if offered.
3. Check validity dates on the certificate before travel or admission.

## Step 6: If errors persist

1. Screenshot the exact error message and note the time.
2. Contact the portal help desk or visit the issuing office.
3. Keep the application number for every support request.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Portal not loading from abroad | Geo-restriction or firewall | Use a Nepali network or ask someone in Nepal to submit |
| Fee paid but certificate not issued | Verification pending | Wait 3-5 working days; then escalate with the receipt |
| Name mismatch with passport | Different transliteration | Correct via the issuing office before application |

## FAQ

**Q:** Is the NOC issued online?

**A:** The application is online; collection may require a physical visit depending on the office.

**Q:** How long does NOC approval take?

**A:** Usually a few working days after document verification.

**Q:** Do I need NOC for every study destination?

**A:** Most foreign institutions and embassies ask for it — confirm with the receiving institution first.
