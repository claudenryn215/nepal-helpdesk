## Quick Answers

| Problem | Common cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bank connect top-up fails | Bank server or session issue | Retry after 10 minutes; try card instead of bank connect |
| Card declined | Card limits or 3DS not completed | Check card limit, complete 3DS, try another card |
| Merchant payment error | Merchant integration issue | Wait, retry once; if repeated, pay via another wallet |


## Step 1: Identify the failure type

1. Note the exact error message and the time it failed.
2. Check whether the amount was deducted from your bank/card.
3. Different errors need different fixes — match yours below.

## Step 2: Fix bank connect top-up failures

1. Top-ups via bank connect depend on the bank's gateway — retry after 10 minutes.
2. Keep the Khalti app updated; old versions break bank links.
3. Try topping up with a card instead of bank connect.

## Step 3: Fix card declined errors

1. Check your daily card limit with your bank.
2. Ensure you complete the 3DS verification pop-up.
3. Try a different card, or confirm international usage is enabled if required.

## Step 4: Check wallet limits and KYC

1. Open Settings > KYC in Khalti and check your verification level.
2. Unverified wallets have low transaction limits — upgrade KYC.
3. If you are at the limit, split the payment across days.

## Step 5: Fix merchant payment failures

1. Many failures are on the merchant side, not your wallet.
2. Wait 5 minutes and try once more.
3. If it still fails, pay with eSewa or bank transfer instead, then dispute the charge with the merchant.

## Step 6: When to contact Khalti support

1. Use the in-app 'Help & Support' with the transaction ID (starts with 'txn-').
2. If money was deducted but not credited, report within 48 hours.
3. Call or email via khalti.com/help with the error screenshot.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Khalti app shows 'something went wrong' | App or session issue | Force close, restart app, or reinstall (KYC data stays on server) |
| OTP from bank not arriving | Bank SMS issues | Use the bank's own app OTP if configured; check SMS inbox spam |
| Refund not appearing | Merchant refund processing time | Merchants refund in 3-7 working days; check after that |

## FAQ

**Q:** How long does a Khalti refund take?

**A:** Typically 3–7 working days after the merchant initiates it.

**Q:** Why is my Khalti transaction limit low?

**A:** Limits depend on your KYC level — upgrade in the app to raise them.

**Q:** Does Khalti charge for top-ups?

**A:** Wallet top-ups via bank/card generally do not carry extra charges; merchant fees are shown at checkout.
