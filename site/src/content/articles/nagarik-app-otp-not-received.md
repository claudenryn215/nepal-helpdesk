## Quick Answers

| Problem | Common cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OTP SMS not arriving | SIM registered under different name/number, or SMS blocked | Verify SIM in your name, re-request OTP, check spam |
| OTP arrives but app rejects it | Expired OTP or wrong phone linked to NID | Request a fresh OTP within 2 minutes |
| 'Service unavailable' error | Server overload (peak hours) | Retry early morning; use LTE/4G, not WiFi from abroad |


## Step 1: Check the SIM and phone number

1. The SIM must be registered in your own name (matches NID records).
2. Confirm your phone number is linked to the same NID at the ward office.
3. If not, update the number at the nearest local body office before retrying.

## Step 2: Force-close and reopen the app

1. Close the Nagarik App completely from recent apps.
2. Turn airplane mode on for 10 seconds, then off.
3. Reopen the app and request OTP again.

## Step 3: Clear the app cache

1. Android: Settings > Apps > Nagarik App > Storage > Clear Cache.
2. Do NOT clear data unless you want to log in fresh.
3. Restart the app and request OTP.

## Step 4: Re-request OTP within the window

1. OTPs expire in about 2 minutes.
2. Enter it immediately when the SMS arrives.
3. If it is rejected, request a fresh one — old codes never work.

## Step 5: Avoid VPN and foreign networks

1. The app checks the SIM network; VPNs or roaming often break verification.
2. Switch off VPN, use Nepali 4G/LTE on the registered SIM.
3. Disable call-forwarding features that may eat the SMS.

## Step 6: Escalate to support

1. Try again after 10:00 PM or before 8:00 AM — servers are less loaded.
2. If the issue persists for days, visit the nearest local body office or call the NID help desk with your NID number.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| App stuck on 'please wait' after OTP | Server overload or outdated app | Update the app from Play Store / App Store, retry later |
| App crashes on open | Old Android version or corrupted install | Update Android WebView, reinstall the app |
| No option for foreigners | App requires NID-linked SIM | Use the portal version on nagarikapp.gov.np with passport details where supported |

## FAQ

**Q:** How long is the Nagarik App OTP valid?

**A:** About 2 minutes. If it expires, request a new OTP rather than re-entering the old one.

**Q:** Can I use Nagarik App with a SIM not in my name?

**A:** No. The SIM must be registered in your own name for verification to pass.

**Q:** Is Nagarik App free?

**A:** Yes, the app and digital documents are free; you only pay for printed documents when requested.
