## Quick Answers

| Problem | Common cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Modem online light off | Cable signal issue or outage in area | Reseat coaxial cable and check outage status |
| WiFi connects but no internet | Modem needs restart or MAC bind issue | Restart modem, wait 5 minutes, then test |
| Very slow uploads | Coaxial signal (SNR) below standard | Ask support to check SNR and power levels |


## Step 1: Restart the cable modem

1. Unplug the modem power adapter.
2. Wait 2 minutes.
3. Plug back in and wait for all lights to stabilize (up to 5 minutes).

## Step 2: Check the coaxial cable

1. Tighten the screw connector at the modem and the wall splitter.
2. Check the splitter — too many splitters weaken the signal.
3. Avoid bending the cable sharply near windows.

## Step 3: Check the modem status page

1. Open a browser and visit `192.168.100.1` (cable modems) or `192.168.1.1`.
2. Look at the downstream/upstream power levels and SNR.
3. If SNR is below ~30 dB or power is far from range, report it to support.

## Step 4: Reset the modem

1. Insert a pin in the reset hole for 10 seconds.
2. Wait for reboot, reconnect WiFi.
3. Re-enter the WiFi password shown on the sticker.

## Step 5: Call Subisu support

1. Call **01-5970750** (Subisu customer care).
2. Give your account number and describe the modem light pattern.
3. Ask for a signal (RSL/SNR) check from their side.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Internet stops after rain | Water in cable line or connector | Request technician visit; avoid DIY outdoor cable work |
| Modem overheats and drops | Ventilation issue | Place modem upright in open space, clean dust |
| Only some sites load | DNS or routing issue | Set DNS 1.1.1.1 and 8.8.8.8 in modem settings |

## FAQ

**Q:** Does Subisu support WiFi 6 routers?

**A:** Yes, if your modem supports it or you use your own router in bridge mode.

**Q:** How do I check Subisu outage in my area?

**A:** Call 01-5970750 or check their Facebook page; major outages are usually announced.

**Q:** Can I change my Subisu WiFi password?

**A:** Yes, log in at 192.168.100.1 or 192.168.1.1 with the sticker credentials and change under Wireless settings.
