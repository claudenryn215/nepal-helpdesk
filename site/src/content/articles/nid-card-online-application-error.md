## Quick Answers

| Problem | Common cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photo upload keeps failing | File too large or wrong format | Use JPEG under 100 KB, 200x200 px, clear background |
| 'Already registered' message | A previous NID enrollment exists | Use the 'forgot' flow with your old details, or visit district office |
| Page won't load / error 500 | Server load or old browser | Use Chrome on a PC with stable internet, retry off-peak |


## Step 1: Prepare correct documents and files

1. Keep your citizenship scan (front and back) ready as JPEG/PDF.
2. Prepare a passport-size photo: clear background, no shadow.
3. Keep your signature image small in size (under 100 KB is safest).

## Step 2: Use the right browser and connection

1. Use the latest Chrome or Edge on a PC or laptop.
2. Disable VPN and ad-blockers — they break the portal.
3. Use a stable internet connection; avoid mobile hotspot if possible.

## Step 3: Fix photo/signature upload errors

1. Resize the photo to exactly 200x200 pixels (JPEG).
2. Ensure file size is within the portal limit (check the help text).
3. Upload signature as JPEG too, then preview before submitting.

## Step 4: Handle 'already registered' errors

1. Select 'Already applied?' and try the search option with your details.
2. If you applied at a ward office, get your application number from them.
3. Visit the district NID office with citizenship if the issue persists.

## Step 5: Complete payment correctly

1. Payment is done via the portal — use eSewa, Khalti, or bank link shown.
2. If payment fails, wait 10 minutes before retrying — do not pay twice.
3. Keep the payment receipt; it proves your application.

## Step 6: Track application status

1. Log in to the portal and check status under 'My Application'.
2. Inquiries take a few weeks; the card is delivered via ward office.
3. If status shows 'rejected', correct the reason shown and re-apply.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OTP not received for NID portal | Number mismatch with NID records | Update phone number at your local body first |
| Payment deducted but application not submitted | Session timeout during payment | Contact the NID help desk with the receipt within 7 days |
| Site shows maintenance | Scheduled upgrades | Retry after a few hours; the portal announces downtime on its site |

## FAQ

**Q:** Is the NID application fee paid online?

**A:** Yes, the online application fee is paid through the portal using local payment methods; printed cards have separate charges.

**Q:** Can I apply for NID without a citizenship card?

**A:** No, citizenship is the primary document for NID enrollment.

**Q:** How long does the NID card take to arrive?

**A:** Usually a few weeks after approval; delivery is through your ward office.
