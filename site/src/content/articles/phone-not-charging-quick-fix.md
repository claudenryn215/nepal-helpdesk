## Quick Answers

| Problem | Common cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Phone doesn't charge at all | Dust in port, bad cable, or software freeze | Clean port gently, test another cable, force restart |
| Charges very slowly | Weak charger or background apps | Use the original charger, enable airplane mode while charging |
| Charges only when off | Battery calibration or hardware issue | Calibrate battery; if persists, visit a service center |


## Step 1: Check the cable and adapter first

1. Try another cable and adapter — most 'charging problems' are cable problems.
2. Check for bent pins, frayed ends, or a burnt smell.
3. Use the original or a quality branded charger (fast charging needs compatible wattage).

## Step 2: Clean the charging port

1. Power off the phone.
2. Use a wooden toothpick or plastic pick to gently lift lint and dust.
3. Never use metal pins or blow hard into the port — it can damage contacts.

## Step 3: Force restart the phone

1. Android: hold power button 10-20 seconds until it restarts.
2. iPhone: press and release volume up, then volume down, hold side button.
3. Plug in after restart and check the battery icon.

## Step 4: Test charging in safe mode

1. Android: hold power, tap-and-hold 'Power off' and confirm Safe Mode.
2. If it charges in safe mode, an app is draining the battery fast.
3. Uninstall recently added apps and reboot normally.

## Step 5: Calibrate the battery

1. Drain the phone until it switches off by itself.
2. Charge to 100% without interruption.
3. Repeat once; this fixes wrong battery percentages, not hardware faults.

## Step 6: When to see a repair shop

1. If none of the above works, the charging port, battery, or charging IC may need service.
2. Get a quote from at least two shops (New Road, Kathmandu, has many options).
3. Ask for genuine battery replacement with warranty.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Charging port smells burnt | Short circuit or moisture | Stop charging immediately; visit a repair shop |
| Charges then stops | Faulty cable or loose port | Replace cable; check port connection tightness |
| Battery drains fast after update | Post-update indexing | Wait 2-3 days; if still bad, clear app caches |

## FAQ

**Q:** Is it safe to charge my phone overnight?

**A:** Modern phones manage charging; overnight charging is generally safe but may wear the battery faster.

**Q:** Why does my phone charge only when switched off?

**A:** Often a software or battery calibration issue; try the steps above before hardware repairs.

**Q:** How much does phone battery replacement cost in Nepal?

**A:** It varies by model and shop; compare quotes from at least two service centers.
