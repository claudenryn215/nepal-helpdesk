## Quick Answers

| Problem | Common cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verification rejected | Blurry documents or name mismatch | Re-upload clear scans matching your PAN/bank name |
| Bank details not accepted | Account name mismatch | Use the bank account in the exact business name |
| Seller Center login loop | Session/cookie issue | Clear cookies, try Chrome incognito, or reset password |


## Step 1: Register your seller account

1. Go to seller.daraz.com.np and click 'Become a Seller'.
2. Register with your business email and phone.
3. Choose the correct account type: personal or company.

## Step 2: Prepare verification documents

1. Citizenship/passport scan (front and back, clear).
2. PAN card or company registration certificate.
3. Bank account details — the account name must match the business name exactly.

## Step 3: Upload documents correctly

1. Use clear, uncropped scans in the format Daraz asks for.
2. All text must be readable; no reflections or shadows.
3. Upload the same documents for seller verification as for payout verification.

## Step 4: Fix the most common rejection reasons

1. Name mismatch: your bank and PAN must match the seller account name.
2. Blurry scan: re-photograph in daylight, flat surface.
3. Expired document: check the validity date before uploading.

## Step 5: Verify bank account for payouts

1. Go to Seller Center > Finance > Bank Accounts.
2. Add the bank account and enter the exact account holder name.
3. If verification is done via micro-deposit, wait 1-2 working days and confirm the amount.

## Step 6: Get unstuck on login loops

1. Clear browser cache and cookies.
2. Use the latest Chrome; disable extensions.
3. Reset the password via the 'Forgot' link, then log in fresh.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verification 'in review' for weeks | Backlog or document issue | Contact Daraz seller support from Seller Center with your application ID |
| Cannot change bank account after rejection | Pending state | Wait for the rejection reason, then edit — do not create duplicate accounts |
| Phone number not accepted | Number used on another account | Use a different number or recover the old account |

## FAQ

**Q:** How long does Daraz seller verification take?

**A:** Usually a few working days after submission.

**Q:** Can I sell on Daraz as an individual?

**A:** Yes, individuals can sell with citizenship and PAN.

**Q:** When do Daraz payouts happen?

**A:** Payouts follow the settlement schedule in Seller Center — usually weekly after order completion.
