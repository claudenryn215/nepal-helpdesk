## Quick Answers

| Problem | Common cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KYC stuck on 'under review' | Document quality or backlogs | Re-submit with clear scans; wait up to 3 working days |
| Selfie verification mismatch | Photo vs document face difference | Retake selfie in good light, same day, no filters |
| Document rejected instantly | Blurry or expired documents | Scan properly, check document validity, re-upload |


## Step 1: Check your KYC level needs

1. Open the eSewa app and go to Profile > KYC.
2. See which level you are on (Basic/Full) and what is required.
3. Full KYC usually needs citizenship/passport plus a selfie.

## Step 2: Prepare documents correctly

1. Use the original citizenship/passport — no copies of copies.
2. Keep the document fully visible, all four corners, no reflection.
3. File size and format: follow the app's on-screen requirements.

## Step 3: Take the selfie properly

1. Good daylight, no glasses or cap.
2. Face must match the ID document — same hair length/style helps.
3. No filters, no editing, straight-facing camera.

## Step 4: Resubmit without spamming

1. If rejected, read the reason and fix that specific point.
2. Resubmitting identical files will fail again — change the document quality.
3. Repeated same-day attempts may trigger a cooldown; wait a day.

## Step 5: What 'under review' means

1. It usually clears within 1–3 working days.
2. Review happens on business days only.
3. If it stays longer than a week, contact eSewa support with your registered number.

## Step 6: Escalate with the right channel

1. eSewa help: 166-00-15000 or esewa.com.np/support.
2. Share your eSewa ID and the exact rejection reason.
3. For unresolved cases, file a complaint at the Nepal Rastra Bank (Nepal Rastra Bank helpline for digital wallet issues).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Can't find KYC section | Old app version | Update the eSewa app from the store |
| Name mismatch between NID and eSewa | Registered name differs from document | Update your name in the app first (requires support approval) |
| Mobile number changed | Number swap breaks verification | Contact support with the old number details to transfer the account |

## FAQ

**Q:** How long does eSewa KYC take?

**A:** Usually 1–3 working days if documents are clear.

**Q:** Can I use eSewa without full KYC?

**A:** Yes, with limited transaction limits; full KYC raises the limits.

**Q:** Is KYC mandatory for eSewa?

**A:** Yes, wallet rules from Nepal Rastra Bank require KYC for wallet services.
