## Quick Answers

| Problem | Common cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Form doesn't submit | Missing field or expired session | Fill all fields, use Chrome, restart if session expired |
| Payment fails at bank/eSewa | Portal or gateway timeout | Retry after 10 minutes; keep the failed transaction ID |
| Application shows duplicate | A previous application exists | Check status with your application number before re-applying |


## Step 1: Check prerequisites

1. You need a valid NID or citizenship number.
2. For renewals, keep the old license number handy.
3. Prepare a passport-size photo in the portal's required format.

## Step 2: Fill the form carefully

1. Use the latest Chrome or Edge browser.
2. Fill every required field — mismatched spelling causes rejections.
3. Double-check your NID number before submitting.

## Step 3: Upload photo and signature

1. Use JPEG files within the size limit shown on the portal.
2. Face must be clearly visible with plain background.
3. Preview the upload before final submission.

## Step 4: Pay the license fee

1. Payment goes through the portal gateway (bank, eSewa, or Khalti).
2. Complete payment within the session window.
3. If it fails, wait 10 minutes before retrying — never double-pay.

## Step 5: Book the written exam slot

1. After payment, select a written exam (license test) date.
2. Choose a district with availability; slots open periodically.
3. Print the exam slip — bring it with your citizenship on exam day.

## Step 6: Track and troubleshoot status

1. Check status at dotm.gov.np using the application number.
2. If 'rejected', read the reason and correct it in a re-application.
3. For portal bugs, email the DoTM help desk with the application number.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No SMS confirmation after application | Number not updated in DoTM records | Update the phone number at the transport office or use the portal lookup |
| Photo rejected for being too dark | Poor lighting | Retake in daylight against a white wall, re-upload |
| Exam date not shown for my district | District exam schedule | Check the DoTM exam calendar; dates are announced per district |

## FAQ

**Q:** Can I apply for a driving license online without NID?

**A:** The portal requires your NID or citizenship details for verification.

**Q:** What is the license application fee?

**A:** Fees vary by category and are displayed before payment on the portal.

**Q:** My payment failed — will I be charged again?

**A:** No, failed payments are not charged. If deducted, the refund comes via the gateway within a few days.
