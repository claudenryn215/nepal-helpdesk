## Quick Answers

| Problem | Common cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Internet slow on WiFi, fast on LAN | Weak signal or channel congestion | Use 5 GHz band or change WiFi channel |
| No internet light | ONT or cable fault | Power cycle ONT and router; reseat LAN cable |
| Frequent disconnects | Router overheating or outdated firmware | Move router to open area, update firmware |


## Step 1: Restart the ONT and router

1. Unplug both the ONT and the WiFi router.
2. Wait 2 minutes, plug in the ONT first, then the router.
3. Test speed on both LAN cable and WiFi.

## Step 2: Connect to the router admin page

1. Connect to the Vianet WiFi network.
2. Open a browser and go to `192.168.1.1`.
3. Enter the credentials from the router sticker.

## Step 3: Switch to 5 GHz and change the channel

1. Go to Wireless Settings.
2. Enable the 5 GHz band and give it a separate name (SSID).
3. Set 2.4 GHz channel to 1, 6 or 11 — the least crowded.

## Step 4: Check connected devices

1. In admin page, open DHCP Clients list.
2. Disconnect devices you do not recognize (possible password sharing).
3. Change the WiFi password to a strong one if unknown devices are present.

## Step 5: Update router firmware

1. In admin page, go to System Tools > Firmware Upgrade.
2. Click check for updates and confirm.
3. Wait 3–5 minutes; do not unplug during update.

## Step 6: Call Vianet support

1. Call **01-5970711** (Vianet call center).
2. Mention your customer ID and speed test results.
3. Ask them to check the OLT port and line signal (RSL).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Speed lower than plan at all times | Plan limits or line signal issue | Run speedtest.net with LAN cable, share results with support |
| WiFi drops at night | Neighbor interference | Change channel to 1/6/11 and enable auto channel selection |
| One device slow, others fine | Device issue, not connection | Forget network, rejoin; update device drivers |

## FAQ

**Q:** What is Vianet's default router password?

**A:** It is printed on the router sticker (often admin / admin). Never leave it default — change it in admin settings.

**Q:** Why is my Vianet internet slow at peak hours?

**A:** Shared bandwidth congestion in your area; request a higher plan or business plan for dedicated bandwidth.

**Q:** Can I use my own router with Vianet fiber?

**A:** Yes, configure it in bridge or PPPoE mode with your Vianet username and password, or ask support for credentials.
