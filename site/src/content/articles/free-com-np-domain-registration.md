## Quick Answers

| Problem | Common cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Application rejected | Blurry citizenship scan or missing nameservers | Re-scan clearly and configure nameservers before applying |
| Nameserver not accepted | Nameservers not resolvable | Use hosting-provider nameservers that resolve publicly |
| Slow approval | Manual verification by Mercantile | Expect 1–3 business days; weekends don't count |


## Step 1: Prepare what you need

1. A valid citizenship card (front and back scan) or passport for foreigners.
2. A working email address.
3. Two nameservers from your hosting provider (e.g., ns1.example.com / ns2.example.com).

## Step 2: Create an account at register.com.np

1. Visit `register.com.np` (Mercantile Communications — the only registrar for .np).
2. Sign up with your real email and contact details.
3. Confirm your email before continuing.

## Step 3: Search for your domain

1. Use the search box to check availability.
2. Good names are still available — try short business or project names.
3. You can register `.com.np`, `.org.np`, `.net.np` etc. per your eligibility.

## Step 4: Fill the application accurately

1. Enter your name, address, and phone exactly as on your ID.
2. Choose your purpose (business, organization, or personal).
3. Provide the two nameservers — this is the #1 rejection reason when missing.

## Step 5: Upload documents and submit

1. Upload the citizenship scan — clear, uncropped, all four corners visible.
2. Use JPEG or PDF as required; blurry scans get rejected.
3. Submit and note the application/reference number.

## Step 6: After approval: point DNS and renew

1. Approval takes 1–3 business days; you will get an email.
2. Configure your records (A, CNAME) at the hosting provider after approval.
3. .com.np is free to renew — but you must renew on time or it is released.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Application stuck for over a week | Manual queue backlog | Email hostmaster@mercantile.com.np with your application number |
| Domain registered to someone else with similar name | First-come policy | Try a variation or different second-level (org.np vs com.np) |
| Ownership transfer | You need both parties | Contact Mercantile with signed documents from current and new owner |

## FAQ

**Q:** Is .com.np really free?

**A:** Yes — registration and renewal are free for Nepali citizens and organizations; you only pay for hosting.

**Q:** Can foreigners register .com.np domains?

**A:** Foreigners with a valid passport and a Nepal-related purpose can apply; approval is at the registrar's discretion.

**Q:** How long does .com.np approval take?

**A:** Usually 1–3 business days because verification is manual.

**Q:** Can I transfer my .com.np to GoDaddy?

**A:** No — .np domains stay with Mercantile; you can change nameservers and ownership with their approval.
