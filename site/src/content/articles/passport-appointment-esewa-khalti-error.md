## Quick Answers

| Problem | Common cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Payment page not loading after form | Browser pop-up/session issue | Clear cookies, disable blockers, retry in Chrome |
| eSewa/Khalti payment declined | Token expired or wallet limit | Re-enter payment within 15 minutes; check wallet balance |
| Paid but no appointment shown | Callback timeout on the portal | Wait 30 minutes, refresh; keep the receipt |


## Step 1: Before starting: check eligibility and slots

1. Confirm you have a valid NID or citizenship number.
2. Check appointment slots — schedule when districts show availability.
3. Have your passport-size photo ready as JPEG (per portal spec).

## Step 2: Fill the application in one sitting

1. Complete the form within the session time to avoid expiry.
2. Save the application number as soon as it is generated.
3. If the session expires, resume with the application number.

## Step 3: Pay via eSewa or Khalti correctly

1. At the payment step, choose eSewa or Khalti.
2. You will be redirected to the wallet login; complete within 15 minutes.
3. Ensure your wallet has enough balance — the passport fee varies by type.

## Step 4: If payment fails, do this first

1. Do NOT retry immediately — wait 10 minutes to avoid double charges.
2. Clear browser cache/cookies and disable ad-block.
3. Try the other payment method (eSewa vs Khalti) if one fails.

## Step 5: Payment deducted but no appointment?

1. Wait 30 minutes and refresh the 'My Application' page.
2. If still missing, screenshot the receipt and the application number.
3. Contact the DoIA help desk (email on nepalpassport.gov.np) with both.

## Step 6: Print appointment slip

1. Once payment reflects, open the appointment schedule.
2. Select your district and preferred date.
3. Print or screenshot the appointment slip — required at the counter.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Portal says 'session expired' | Idle too long | Restart from login with the same application number |
| Khalti balance deducted twice | Double click on pay | File a refund request in the wallet app; one will be reversed |
| No appointment slots in my district | Slots open weekly | Check again on Sunday/Monday mornings; slots open in batches |

## FAQ

**Q:** How much is the passport application fee?

**A:** Fees change with passport type and urgency; the portal shows the exact amount before payment.

**Q:** Can I pay for someone else's passport?

**A:** Yes, if you log in to their application with the application number, but the biometric appointment must be theirs.

**Q:** What if my payment fails at the last step?

**A:** Wait 10 minutes and retry; if the amount was deducted, the portal shows it after refreshing.
